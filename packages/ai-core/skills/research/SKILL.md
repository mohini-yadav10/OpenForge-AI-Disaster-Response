# Deep Research

## Description
Conducts multi-step research by searching knowledge bases and synthesizing complex findings.

## Purpose
Comprehensive data gathering.

## When to Use
research, investigate, deep dive, explore, find out about

## Tools
- web_search
- document_search

## Instructions
1. Break down the user's research request into sub-queries.
2. Search multiple sources.
3. Compare and contrast the findings.

## Input
A broad research topic.

## Output
A detailed research report with cited sources and an executive summary.

## Examples
- Research the impact of transformer models on robotics.
- Investigate the competitive landscape of AI open-source frameworks.
